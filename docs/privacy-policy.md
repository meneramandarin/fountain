**Effective date: July 9, 2026**

Fountain ("Fountain," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Play by Ear LLC, a Delaware limited liability company. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit fountain.clinic (the "Site") and how we use it.

## What We Collect

Fountain does not require an account, and we do not ask you to submit personal information to use the Site. The information we collect is limited to the following.

### Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics 4 ("GA4") to understand how visitors use the Site. GA4 collects information automatically through cookies and similar technologies, including your approximate location (derived from your IP address), device and browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring website, and interactions with links on the Site, including clicks on outbound links to third-party clinic and booking websites.

This data is aggregated and does not directly identify you. Google processes this information on our behalf. You can learn more about how Google handles data at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using the browser add-on available at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

### Correspondence

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message. We use this only to respond to you.

## How We Use Information

We use analytics data to operate and improve the Site, understand which content and listings are useful to visitors, and measure traffic to third-party websites we link to. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Third-Party Websites

The Site contains links to third-party websites, including clinic websites and booking platforms. When you click an outbound link, we record the click through GA4 for analytics purposes. Once you leave the Site, the privacy practices of the destination website apply, and we encourage you to review their policies. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.

## Cookies

The Site uses cookies set by GA4 for the analytics purposes described above. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to use the Site.

## Data Retention

Analytics data is retained in accordance with our GA4 configuration and Google's retention policies. Email correspondence is retained as long as needed to respond to and follow up on your inquiry.
